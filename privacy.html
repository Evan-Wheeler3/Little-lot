Little Lot privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version

Little Lot does not show ads, does not track you, and does not sell or share your data. You can play without an account. If you make one, we keep only what is needed to save your game.

Playing without an account

Your progress and settings are stored on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.

If you create an account

  • Email address and password, used to sign you in and to send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password). Passwords are stored hashed by our database provider, never in plain text.
  • Your game save (money, upgrades, stars and similar game numbers), so you can continue on another device.

This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is used only to run the game. Each account can read and change only its own save.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, the game schedules them on your device (for example when away earnings have filled up). They are not sent from a server.

Deleting your data

Open the game, tap the account button, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and its cloud save. To remove data stored only on your device, use Settings > Reset progress or delete the app.

Children

Little Lot is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. An account is optional.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the contact address in the App Store listing.
